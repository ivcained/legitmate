# LegitClub pre-configured assistants

## Offer

LegitClub provides managed AI assistants that stay available around the clock and are configured for a specific job. Each assistant has a dedicated cloud workspace, approved tools, memory, skills, schedules, and operating rules.

The customer starts with a description of the work. LegitClub turns that description into a reviewable configuration and deploys the appropriate assistant environment after payment and provisioning checks.

## Example presets

### YouTube assistant

A creator-facing assistant for channel operations:

- Topic and audience research
- Video ideas and content calendars
- Title, description, tag, and thumbnail-brief drafts
- Publishing checklists
- Competitor/channel research
- Performance review summaries
- Recurring optimization notes
- Sponsorship and collaboration tracking

The customer connects their approved YouTube account; the assistant does not claim guaranteed views, subscribers, or rankings.

### WhatsApp assistant

A messaging-first assistant for personal users, creators, and small businesses:

- Dedicated number where available
- WhatsApp activation where eligible
- SMS/phone capability where supported
- Reminders and follow-ups
- Calendar support
- Research and question answering
- Approved message drafts and replies
- Human escalation for sensitive or consequential requests

A purchased number is not automatically WhatsApp-ready. Eligibility, verification, opt-in, templates, and provider rules are checked during activation.

### Business operator

A LegitDesk preset for offices and professional teams:

- Email triage and drafts
- Research and reports
- CRM updates
- Sales follow-up preparation
- Calendar and meetings
- Customer-request triage
- Documents and spreadsheets
- Internal knowledge retrieval
- Website and development support
- Scheduled team updates

## Prompt-to-deployment flow

```text
Customer describes the work
  → system extracts desired role and tasks
  → LegitClub proposes a preset/configuration
  → customer selects plan and provider
  → customer pays
  → workspace and assistant are provisioned
  → integrations are connected with customer authorization
  → test task runs
  → customer approves activation
  → maintenance and usage monitoring begin
```

## Product language

Use:

> Describe the work. We prepare the assistant.

> Start with a working setup, not a blank screen.

> Your assistant arrives with the tools, skills, schedules, and guardrails for the job you chose.

Avoid:

- Unrestricted computer claims
- Guaranteed platform results
- Universal WhatsApp availability
- “Fully autonomous employee”
- “Unlimited” usage
- Claims that the assistant can act without permissions or approvals

## Implementation boundary

The prompt parser may suggest a template and configuration. It must not silently grant permissions, connect accounts, purchase numbers, send messages, publish content, or make purchases. Those actions require an entitlement check and/or explicit customer approval.

## Platform model

- Agent 37 provides the isolated persistent cloud computer.
- LegitClub owns customer accounts, plans, billing, configuration, support, and audit history.
- Approved model providers are selected per customer plan.
- Provider credentials remain server-side.
- Customer-connected accounts are authorized by the customer and scoped to the assistant.
